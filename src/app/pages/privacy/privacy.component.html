Privacy

This site does not set cookies, run analytics, or collect personal data of its own.

  • Hosting: the site is served through Cloudflare, which processes request metadata (such as your IP address) to deliver pages and protect against abuse, under its own privacy policy.
  • Fonts: fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.
  • Status badge: the home page loads an image from updog.dorm.social, which is run by the same people as this site.
  • Browser storage: nothing is stored on your device.
