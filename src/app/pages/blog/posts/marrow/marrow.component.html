After a four year wait, posters in NY and LA, two websites, a bunch of promo TikToks and a TikTok Live, two ARGs, him roleplaying with himself in his Discord Server, and 4 singles, Randall Findell, releases marroW, his most heartfelt project yet. Digging deep into himself, and themes that range from accepting death, environmental guilt and cutting out abusive behaviour out of himself.


ready or noT

On September 25th 2026, brakence, after teasing on us on Twitter, released ready or noT, the first single of this release cycle. I instantly resonated with the lyrics, and the visual and sonic aesthetics of this track. "Homie is going through something", a partner of mine said, after experiencing the music video. This song introduces the physical form, marionettes, for butcheR and jesteR, two of the characters at play in this album. This track alone is enough to let us understand that these two creatures torment Randy's psyche.

The music video features jesteR's disembodied glove slipping a substance into Randy's stew, and butcheR trying to kill jesteR. As this goes on, we see Randy's body get pulled by strings, him grabbing an axe, a pretty on the nose metaphor for this internal conflict. While I will not cover all the material I previously mentioned, and highly suggest you experience it yourself, I will continue giving my interpretation of this record as we go on


crystallized & death rolL

I want you to picture this, someone you dearly love woke you up to listen to the new brakence singles, and track 1 is "crystallized", probably Randy's only love song without bitter notes, that seamlessly flows between comparing his love interest to a muse, and pretty on the nose innuendos about being inside this woman. His voice is smooth like silk and sweet like honey, doing his trademark falsetto, this time, over guitars, synths, and a very percussive beat with an addictive bassline. A really good technique used is tying the absence of those harsh percussive elements to the volume of the melodic elements, like an inverse sidechain.
